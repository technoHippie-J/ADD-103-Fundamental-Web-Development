City Selection

World Map of Eryndor

The world of Eryndor is a vast place with many different cities and landscapes. Choose your city wisely as it will determine your starting location and the types of quests you will encounter.

If you are an orc, you have no choice and will start in the orc city.

If you are a human, elf, or dwarf, you can choose from: the desert city of Dun-Karath, the forest city of Sylvaris, or the ocean city of Tidehaven.

  • Dun-Karath, the desert city
  • Sylvaris, the forest city
  • Tidehaven, the ocean city
  • Ghor'Vash, the orc city
